Treetop Lunch

Treetop, middle branch, or bush: each animal has its own height. Level 1: the hungry animal is picked for you, so just tap the leaves it can reach. Level 2: pick the animal and the leaves yourself, and feed the one shouting “Hungry!” for double points.

Lunch on the savanna

60 seconds. Three mix-ups and the animals wander off.

Tap an animal, then tap the leaves it can reach.

The ruler on the right shows height in metres. Adult giraffes browse acacia leaves up to about 5.5 m off the ground, young giraffes take the lower branches, and zebras eat close to the ground.
