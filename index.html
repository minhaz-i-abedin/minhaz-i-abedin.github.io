Research Experience
2020 — 2022
Optimizing and Benchmarking RRAM Devices for AI Hardware
IBM, Yorktown Heights & University at Albany · Advisors: Takashi Ando, Nanbo Gong (IBM), Dr. Nathaniel Cady

Team developed CMOS-integrated TaOx and HfOx RRAM on the 65nm node. Electrically characterized devices from short (300ps) to long (10µs) pulses across pulse amplitudes, and analyzed switching behavior to benchmark neural-network accuracy with the IBM AIHWKit simulator.

2020 — 2021
Predicting COVID-19 Infection with RRAM-enabled Neuromorphic Hardware
University at Albany · Advisors: Dr. James Plank (UT Knoxville), Dr. Nathaniel Cady

Prepared multiplexed antibody biosensor data to train a small neural network; trained ANN and SNN models with 6-bit quantization and collected experimental inference data on an 8×8 CMOS-integrated RRAM array.

2020 — 2021
Peripheral Circuits for 65nm RRAM 1T1R Arrays for In-Memory Computation
University at Albany · Advisors: Yu Cao, Jae-sun Seo (ASU), Jaydeep Kulkarni (UT Austin), Dr. Nathaniel Cady

Created Verilog models of fabricated multilevel RRAM devices with variation; designed the micro-architecture for 64×64 CMOS-integrated RRAM cells and peripheral circuits for single and multiple selection with level shifters and analog MUX.

2018 — 2020
Microfluidic Chips for Optical Ring Resonator Sensors
University at Albany · AIM Photonics · Advisors: Dr. Benjamin Miller (U. Rochester), Dr. Nathaniel Cady

Developed polymer microfluidic chips and integrated them with silicon photonic biosensor chips for packaged Lab-on-a-Chip applications.

2014 — 2015
Pixel Circuits for Wide Dynamic Range CMOS Image Sensors
Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology · Advisor: Dr. Quazi Delwar Hossain

Researched novel image-sensor pixel circuits using fewer transistors while achieving higher sensitivity.
