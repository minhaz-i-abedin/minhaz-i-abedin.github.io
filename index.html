Research Interests

⬡

Semiconductor Devices & Interconnects

Design-technology co-optimization of gate-all-around nanosheet logic at the 2nm node and next-generation CFET architectures — balancing transistor, interconnect, and standard-cell trade-offs across performance, yield, reliability, and area.

◈

RRAM for AI Hardware

Developing CMOS-integrated TaOx and HfOx resistive RAM on 65nm platforms for energy-efficient neuromorphic and in-memory computing, benchmarked against neural network accuracy targets.

⬡

AI Accelerators & 3D / Chiplet Integration

System technology co-optimization for AI hardware: PPA assessment for target LLM workloads (e.g., Llama, Granite), chiplet-based heterogeneous integration, silicon-interposer packaging, and thermal / structural / power / signal-integrity sign-off.

◎

Neuromorphic Computing

Hardware-aware neural network training and inference on RRAM arrays — ANN and SNN with 6-bit quantization, in-memory error-correcting codes and image processing, and peripheral circuit design for 64×64 1T1R arrays in 65nm.

◇

Qubit Device Fabrication

Integration studies for fabricating superconducting qubits in a silicon wafer process, working with process, integration, and architecture teams on next-generation superconducting quantum systems.

⬡

Lab-on-a-Chip Photonics

Polymer microfluidic integration with silicon photonic biosensors for cost-effective clinical diagnostics, including COVID-19 antibody detection platforms.
